Modules

Fifteen modules. One shared record.

Every module below is built into PeopleHub and reads from the same employee and organizational data — nothing is re-entered twice.

Login & Security

  • Email/User ID login with show/hide password and remember-me
  • Google & Microsoft SSO, plus MFA via email, SMS, or WhatsApp
  • reCAPTCHA, lockout after 3 failed attempts, IP-based rate limiting

Subscription & Multi-Tenancy

  • 5-step guided tenant creation wizard for new organizations
  • Dedicated database schema auto-created per tenant
  • Centralized tenant management restricted to Super Admins

Roles & Access Control

  • Granular, module-level permission matrix per role
  • Standard, MFA-required, or approval-based access tiers
  • Field-level security to hide or show sensitive data by role

Organization Structure

  • Multi-level entities — departments, divisions, branches, subsidiaries
  • Tree and list views with legal & registration details per entity
  • Headcount, vacancy, and workforce distribution tracking

Organization Hierarchy

  • Dynamic role hierarchies based on reporting levels
  • Role-specific qualifications, skills, and responsibilities
  • Reusable role profiles that feed straight into recruitment

Employee Master

  • Complete digital profile — identity, contact, compensation, documents
  • Encrypted storage for sensitive employee fields
  • Bulk create and update via Excel upload

Time & Attendance

  • Configurable work patterns and attendance calculation rules
  • Biometric & access-control device integration via WDMS
  • Custom formula builder for shifts, policies, and roles

Leave Management

  • Configurable leave policies and multi-level approval workflows
  • Real-time balance visibility for employees and managers
  • Absence reporting tied directly into payroll

Payroll Management

  • Automated salary calculations, deductions, and statutory tax handling
  • Direct attendance & leave integration for accurate runs
  • Payslip generation and earnings/deductions configuration

Employee Lifecycle

  • Transfers, promotions, and career progression tracking
  • Loan requests, repayment tracking, and benefit enrollment
  • Full employment history maintained against every profile

Employee Termination

  • Structured offboarding and exit workflow
  • Disciplinary action and incident record keeping
  • Final settlement handling tied to payroll and assets

Recruitment

  • Vacancy posting and candidate pipeline tracking
  • Interview coordination and digital onboarding workflows
  • Automated onboarding task assignment for new hires

Learning & Development

  • Training catalog with learning schedules and course assignment
  • Progress tracking against role-specific skill requirements
  • Continuous learning culture built into the employee profile

Succession Planning

  • High-potential talent identification and readiness assessments
  • Leadership pipeline tracking against critical roles
  • Structured development planning for future leaders

Performance Management

  • Goal management and OKRs tied to organizational objectives
  • Continuous feedback and 360-degree evaluations
  • Structured review cycles feeding succession & L&D

Intelligence, Security & Tech

Insight, governance, and a modern foundation

The engine room: role-based access, audited authentication, and the technology platform underneath every module.

Authentication Security

JWT authentication with configurable token expiration, protected API endpoints, and OTP verification before access is granted.

Account Protection

Lockout after 3 failed logins, IP-based rate limiting at 5 attempts, and reCAPTCHA on login & password recovery.

Audit & Monitoring

Device, browser, OS, and approximate location captured on every login, with full audit logs for compliance reporting.

Role-Based Governance

Granular permissions, approval-based access tiers, and field-level security across every module.
