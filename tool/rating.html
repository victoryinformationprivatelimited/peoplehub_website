How the score works

  • Wins and revenue · 40
    Monthly value you have won, and how many deals, in the last 12 months.
  • Win rate · 15
    Won against lost. Neutral until you have a few decisions.
  • Follow-up habit · 15
    Follow-ups finished on or before the due date.
  • Pipeline activity · 15
    Leads registered and proposals sent, last 90 days.
  • Engagement · 15
    Presentations given and calls or meetings logged, last 90 days.
